OneDeck Ports

Xbox classics rebuilt to run natively on PC. Install them from OneDeck with your own copy of the game, and they update themselves.

Some features below arrive with the next update, out with OneDeck itself. No game files are included: bring your own.

Star Wars: Obi Comp

A PC port of Star Wars: Obi-Wan (Xbox, 2001)

Alpha
  • True widescreen: the game draws the wider view itself, nothing pops in at the edges
  • Smooth frame rate, with the stutters and lighting glitches fixed
  • Jedi Battle for two players
  • Any-Wan: play Battle Royale as Qui-Gon, Mace Windu or six other Jedi, with their look, saber colour and Force abilities
  • Maul vs Maul: fight Darth Maul as Darth Maul
  • Cheats: unlock every chapter and fighter, invincibility, unlimited Force
GitHub page

Komp Fu Chaos

A PC port of Kung Fu Chaos (Xbox, 2003)

Alpha
  • Up to four players on one screen
  • Sharp, full-resolution fights at 60 fps
  • Cheat: unlock every mode, scene and character at once
GitHub page

Crash Nitro Komp

A PC port of Crash Nitro Kart (Xbox, 2003)

Beta
  • Widescreen, or 4:3 as on an old TV
  • Full sound: every voice line and finish-line jingle, and 3D engine sounds
  • Cheats: infinite Wumpa fruit, infinite boost, the best crate items, unlock everything, no rubberbanding
GitHub page

A settings menu

F1 or Back + Start, mid-game: display, resolution, frame rate, sound, controls and cheats.

Keyboard, mouse or pad

Xbox-style controllers work as they are, and every key can be rebound.

Mods and texture packs

Drop files in a folder beside the game to replace its own.

Windows and Linux

Native on Windows; OneDeck runs them through Proton on Linux.

OneDeck doesn't include any games or system files: bring your own. Not affiliated with any console maker.
